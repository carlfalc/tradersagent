How RON trades — the engine
RON builds a separate high-probability playbook for every session — Sydney, Tokyo/Asian, London, the overlap and New York — learning each one's character and hunting the best setups in all of them. The goal: find the highest-probability edge available right now, in whatever session is live, and press it.
01 · SEE
Reads every candle
Trend, structure breaks, support/resistance, volatility, volume and chart patterns — on the 3-minute chart, timestamped in NZT.
02 · LEARN
Grades its own calls
Every pattern and trade scored by what price did next, per session and time of day. The playbook sharpens as the sample grows.
03 · DECIDE
High-probability only
Trades only when conviction clears its learned floor and the setup matches a proven edge for that session.
04 · PROTECT
Defends every trade
Stops outside the spread, winners to break-even then trailing, losing streaks trigger a cool-off.
05 · ADAPT
Rewrites its own rules
Floors, filters and cool-offs are set by RON from the data — and changed the moment the numbers say something different.
